# Fahim AI Brand Identity

## Core idea

Fahim is the verified learning loop: a learner attempts, understands the mistake, follows the next learning path, and proves retention.

The mark combines three ideas in one compact Arabic symbol:

- The body of the Arabic letter **ف** identifies Fahim without relying on a generic AI symbol.
- The teal rising path represents correction and learning progress.
- The pure saffron dot identifies the Arabic letter and represents the moment of understanding. Verification is expressed through the learning journey, not a tiny check inside the dot.

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the diameter of its saffron dot.
- Use the icon alone below 120px wide. Use the full lockup in navigation, decks, documents, and partner material.
- Minimum digital icon size: 20px. Minimum full lockup width: 148px.
- Use the dark lockup only on Fahim Ink or similarly dark solid backgrounds.
- Use the mono lockup for legal, embossing, stamps, and one-color printing.
- Never rotate, stretch, recolor individual pieces, add glow, add a container shape, or place the logo over a busy image without a solid quiet zone.
- The saffron dot belongs to the letter. Do not detach it as a decorative badge or add a checkmark inside it.

## Voice attached to the identity

Primary Arabic line: **الفهم الذي يمكنك إثباته**

English descriptor: **Verified learning**

Use direct educational language. Avoid generic AI claims, model names, inflated superlatives, and unverified outcome claims.
